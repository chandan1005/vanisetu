## Why not Google Translate?

| Google Translate | VANISETU |
|---|---|
| Translates speech | Translates speech, then checks it was understood |
| No safety checks | Confirms doses, timing and duration before translating |
| No understanding check | Teach-back: the patient repeats, VANISETU checks |
| No record | Consultation history with understanding status and timeline |
| General purpose | Built for the doctor–patient conversation |

**Key message:** Google Translate helps people communicate. VANISETU helps make sure patients understand their care.

## What the doctor sees at the end
- Understanding: Understood ✓ / Needs re-explaining
- Communication risk: 🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High (about understanding, not a medical assessment)
- Why it was flagged: what the patient said, what was expected, the reason (for example "Medicine frequency misunderstood")
- Timeline: safety clarification, each teach-back attempt, summary saved
