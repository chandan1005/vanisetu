# VANISETU – 3-minute demo video script

Record in desktop Chrome. Keep it under 3 minutes. Upload to YouTube as Unlisted.

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "In a busy hospital, a patient who doesn't speak the doctor's language may never be understood." | Home |
| 0:12 | "VANISETU lets each person speak their own language." | Pick Hindi patient, English doctor → Start |
| 0:25 | "The patient speaks Hindi; VANISETU shows his words and the translation." | Patient: Hold to speak (or Patient mic) |
| 0:45 | "The doctor says 'two tablets'. That's risky, so VANISETU asks before translating." | Doctor: Hold to speak → Safety check → "1 tablet, two times a day" |
| 1:05 | "Real instructions work too. The doctor dictates with Wispr Flow." | Wispr Flow dictating "Take one tablet twice daily after food for five days" → Doctor said this → chips → Yes |
| 1:30 | "Did the patient understand? He repeats it back." | Check understanding → answer "one tablet at night" → Mismatch (Missed: 2 times a day, after food) |
| 1:50 | "VANISETU explains again. Now he gets it right." | Explain again → "one tablet in the morning and one at night, after food" → Understood |
| 2:10 | "The consultation is saved and can be replayed." | Summary → Save → My Consultations → Details → Replay |
| 2:30 | "Today it runs fully in the browser with no keys. Next: backend, medical-grade AI, missed-call replay on basic phones." | Home status lines |
| 2:50 | "Wispr helps capture what was said. VANISETU makes sure it was understood." | Title |

## Before recording
- Open **My Consultations** once: six sample consultations appear (Low, Medium, High, Not checked, Urgent). Show them for 10 seconds at the end: "Every outcome is recorded."
- Show the insulin sample: "10 ml" instead of "10 units" → High risk, Reason: Dose misunderstood. Judges remember this.
